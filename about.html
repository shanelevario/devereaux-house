About

Devereaux House is a natural wine bar and pizza restaurant in an old house on Nelson Street, in Riverside, CA. The house has been standing here a lot longer than we have, and we're honored to be here.

We pour natural wines, mostly from small producers, and the list moves constantly. Each week, our "by the glass" is different, so you're always trying something new. We also have an extensive bottle list, cocktails, and of course, pizza, made in house by the Inner Pizza team.

There is a plant filled patio out back with live music on Fridays. Our sister restaurant, Backstreet, has the daytime. We take the evenings!

We take reservations for parties of 2 to 12 here, and bigger parties by request. Walk ins are always welcome, we always have room.
